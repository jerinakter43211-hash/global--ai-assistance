# Global AI Assistance

A global, mobile-first assistance platform for everyday problems, education, skills, careers, privacy-first sharing, and country-aware emergency guidance.

## Core modules
- Everyday AI assistance
- Private "share your problem" submissions
- High Alert / Emergency guidance
- Student Hub with image-question workflow
- Skills & Career learning paths
- Jobs & career guidance
- General health information
- Daily Advice (admin approval before publishing)
- Country & language selection
- Central API-ready architecture for web and mobile clients

## Safety
Emergency contact information must come from verified country-specific sources. The app never claims to contact authorities unless an authorized integration actually exists. Location is permission-based and private submissions are never public.
